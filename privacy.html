Privacy policy

Vince Personal Rclone is a personal-use OAuth application operated by Vincent Desjardins. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Google Drive access

The application uses rclone to access the owner's Google Drive files and metadata for local browsing, mounting, reading, uploading, modifying, and synchronizing files. Available access depends on the permissions granted during authorization.

Local storage

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration. Files and metadata may be cached on the owner's computer. This website does not receive OAuth credentials or Google Drive files.

Sharing and use

The operator does not sell Google user data or share it with third parties. Rclone communicates with Google APIs to perform requested operations. Google Drive data is not used for advertising or training AI models.

Retention and control

Local credentials and caches remain until removed by the owner. Google Drive files remain under the owner's control. Access can be revoked through the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Local credentials and cached data can be deleted separately.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this informational website and may process standard access information under its own privacy policy. This site includes no analytics or tracking scripts.

Contact

Contact the developer through the repository. Do not post credentials or private files in public issues.
